# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Students and parents who need homework content neatly handwritten on loose pages and delivered to an address.

## Product Purpose

The service lets a customer submit homework content, choose a handwriting preference, pay with delivery included, and follow the work from order placement through delivery.

## Positioning

The service pairs a simple online submission flow with personalised handwriting by a real writing team, while making the page-only policy explicit before checkout.

## Operating Context

Customers use the site on mobile or desktop to submit PDFs, photos, documents, or pasted text. The team reviews each order in an admin dashboard, writes on individual pages, updates status, and ships the completed work.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Orders collect customer details, homework content, page estimate, subject, class, instructions, handwriting style, ink colour, and optional handwriting sample.
- Multiple files can be uploaded and reviewed before payment.
- Payment supports common Indian online methods in the product flow.
- Delivery charges are visible in the price breakdown and final total.
- Homework is written only on loose or individual pages, never directly in notebooks.
- Customers can track an order with an order ID and mobile number.
- Admins can update order status, add internal notes, review submissions, and view order history.

## Product Principles

- Make the next action obvious.
- Set the page-only expectation before commitment.
- Show the full price before payment.
- Keep progress visible after checkout.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable typography, high-contrast controls, clear labels, keyboard-visible focus, and mobile-first layouts.
