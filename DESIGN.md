# Design system

<!-- impeccable:design-system 1 -->

## Direction

Warm study desk: the interface feels like a well-organised school desk translated into a calm digital service. It refuses the generic delivery-app dashboard by making the page-only promise and work journey the visual anchors.

## Palette

- Ink navy `#172238` for type, navigation, and primary actions.
- Paper `#fbfaf7` for the canvas.
- Coral `#ed6a5a` for action and status emphasis.
- Pencil `#e8e2d8` for dividers and fields.
- Sage `#b9d6c2` for completed / reassuring states.
- Wash `#f1eee8` for quiet panels.

## Typography

Use a readable system sans stack for controls and body copy, with a soft editorial serif for the hero headline. Display copy is compact and confident; supporting copy is conversational and never overly technical.

## Composition

The landing view uses a split hero with a tactile paper order preview, then a generous process ribbon, then the order flow. Sections are anchored with simple numbered sequence markers because the process itself is the content. Forms use wide fields and a sticky summary on larger screens.

## Interaction

Use minimal motion: a single paper-slide entrance on the hero and short state transitions for tabs, upload rows, and the progress tracker. Focus states are coral outlines with a visible offset.
